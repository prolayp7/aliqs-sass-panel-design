Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing cycles, overages, and security.

swap_horiz

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated balance credits. If you decide to downgrade or cancel, your current tier limits remain fully functional until the end of your billing cycle on Nov 12. No cancellation penalties or lock-in contracts.

data_thresholding

What happens if I exceed my conversation quota?

NovaLead never cuts off active leads. We alert Maya Singh and team admins at 80% and 95% capacity. If you reach 100%, you can toggle automatic on-demand conversation packs ($15 per 250 chats) or auto-upgrade without dropping a single visitor session.

verified_user

Is payment processing and telemetry secure?

All financial transactions are tokenized via Stripe with Level 1 PCI-DSS compliance. NovaLead is SOC-2 Type II certified, GDPR-compliant, and encrypts all conversation vectors in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 GCM.

Need custom enterprise volume (>50,000 chats)?

For global organizations handling tens of thousands of conversations per day across multi-brand holding groups, we offer dedicated Kubernetes clusters, private LLM fine-tuning, and custom MSAs. Contact our Solutions Engineering Team →
